How it works

Verification & activation lifecycle

  1. 1
    Submit

    Select your preferred plan and upload proof of payment (bank transfer, JazzCash, or Easypaisa receipt).

  2. 2
    Review

    Super Admin reviews transaction reference and authenticates the payment evidence.

  3. 3
    Activate

    Subscription activates or extends automatically upon review approval.

Submitting evidence queues a review record. Verification by platform administrators is required for activation.
