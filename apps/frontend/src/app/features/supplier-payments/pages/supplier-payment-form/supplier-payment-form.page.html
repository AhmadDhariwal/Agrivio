How it works?

  • General (oldest-first) allocates the payment to the oldest unpaid purchases automatically; any remainder becomes supplier advance.
  • Invoice-specific lets you choose a particular purchase (invoice) to allocate.
  • The supplier ledger and balance update after successful posting.
  • Payments can only use accounts available to the current organization.

Important

Only payments from your organization's accounts are allowed. Make sure the amount and date are correct before posting.
