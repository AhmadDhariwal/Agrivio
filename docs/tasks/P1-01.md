## Product Summary

The product is a cloud-first Fertilizer POS and Inventory Management web application for:

* Fertilizer retailers
* Seed stores
* Pesticide and chemical dealers
* Agricultural-input wholesalers
* Dealers and distributors

The first release will initially serve two clients but must support additional organizations in the future.

## Finalized Technical Decisions

* Frontend: Angular and TypeScript
* Backend: Node.js, Express and TypeScript
* Database: MongoDB with Mongoose
* Repository: Monorepo
* Architecture: Modular monolith
* API style: REST
* Styling: SCSS with a centralized design system
* Deployment: Shared SaaS and dedicated cloud from the same codebase
* On-premise: Contract-only future-compatible deployment
* Release 1 language: English
* Future language: Urdu
* Release 1 currency: PKR
* Offline synchronization is excluded from Release 1
* Native mobile applications are excluded from Release 1

## Finalized Organization Rules

* The system is multi-tenant.
* Every organization has isolated data.
* Public organization activation is admin-approved.
* Super Admin creates or approves organizations.
* Every organization has an Owner.
* The Owner manages employees for their own organization.
* An Owner cannot access another organization.
* Employees may be assigned to one or multiple branches or warehouses.

## Finalized Roles

* Super Admin
* Owner
* Manager
* Cashier
* Store Keeper

Backend authorization is permission-based. Roles are predefined permission bundles. Direct role-name checks are prohibited except for documented platform-scope boundaries such as Super Admin operations.

## Finalized Inventory Rules

* Products support configurable categories and units.
* Inventory is stored in base units.
* Unit conversions are automatic.
* Common quantities include 1, 5, 10, 20, 25, 40 and 50 kilograms.
* Liquid products support configurable volume units.
* Conversion values used in transactions must be historically preserved.
* Batch tracking is mandatory for fertilizers, seeds, pesticides and chemicals.
* Products may use:

  * No batch tracking
  * Batch tracking
  * Batch and expiry tracking
* Batches remain separate for loose stock.
* Expiring products use FEFO.
* FIFO is used as fallback where expiry does not apply.
* Expired-product sales require Manager or Owner approval.
* Expired-product sales require a warning, reason and audit entry.
* Expiry alerts support 30, 60, 90 and custom days.
* Negative stock is blocked by default.
* Owner override may allow negative stock with a mandatory reason and audit entry.
* Warehouses start with one but the architecture supports multiple warehouses.
* Weighted-average inventory cost is maintained by product and warehouse.
* Freight, loading, transport and applicable landed costs are included in average cost.
* Every stock change must have a traceable stock movement.

## Finalized Sales and Customer Rules

* Sales support cash, credit, partial and mixed payments.
* Walk-in credit is controlled by organization policy and permission.
* Anonymous walk-in credit is not allowed.
* Customer credit-limit behaviour is organization-configurable:

  * Warning
  * Manager approval
  * Block
* Customer payments support:

  * Invoice-specific payments
  * General customer payments
* General customer payments allocate automatically to the oldest unpaid invoices.
* Unallocated payment may remain as customer advance.
* Customer type and price tier are separate fields.
* Customer types include:

  * Walk-in
  * Farmer
  * Individual
  * Business
  * Corporate
* Price tiers include:

  * Retail
  * Wholesale
  * Dealer
  * Distributor
* Customer price tier automatically selects product pricing.
* Manual price overrides require permission and audit where applicable.
* Sales returns may be processed with or without an original invoice.
* Returns without an invoice require lookup, approval and audit.

## Finalized Purchase and Supplier Rules

* Purchases support partial and full payments.
* Purchases create stock movements.
* Purchases update supplier payable.
* Supplier payments may be invoice-specific or general.
* Purchase returns update stock, supplier payable and valuation.
* Multi-record purchase workflows must be atomic.

## Finalized Printing and Migration Rules

* Supported print formats:

  * 58 mm thermal
  * 80 mm thermal
  * A4
* Initial printer connections:

  * USB
  * LAN
* Browser-based printing is sufficient for Release 1.
* Data migration supports:

  * Manual entry
  * Excel import
* Import validation must show errors before final import.
* Invalid rows must not be silently ignored.

## Finalized Subscription Decisions

Plans:

* Starter
* Business
* Enterprise

Commercial model:

* Monthly billing
* Annual billing
* PKR in Release 1
* Approximately 15% annual discount
* Fourteen-day approved trial
* No payment method required for trial
* Seven-day grace period
* Manual billing in Release 1
* Bank transfer
* JazzCash
* Easypaisa
* Plan limits enforced through backend entitlements
* Soft warnings before hard creation limits
* Existing data must never be deleted because a plan limit is exceeded
* Shared SaaS is the default deployment
* Dedicated cloud is an Enterprise option
* Client business data belongs to the client
* Product source code and platform intellectual property belong to the provider unless agreed otherwise
* Cancellation and data deletion are separate processes
* Recommended recoverable retention after cancellation is 90 days
