## 8. Gross-Profit Boundary

Replace vague “profit report” language with an explicit Release 1 gross-profit boundary.

Release 1 must calculate gross profit using:

```text
Net sales revenue
- weighted-average cost of goods sold
```

The calculation must account for:

* Product and invoice discounts
* Sales returns
* Reversed or cancelled sales
* Weighted-average cost by product and warehouse

Release 1 does not provide full accounting net profit, balance sheet, trial balance, or general-ledger profit.

The detailed formula belongs in `BUSINESS_RULES.md`.

Requirements and report names should use “Gross Profit” where appropriate rather than ambiguous “Profit.”
