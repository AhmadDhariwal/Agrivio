# 6. Common Transaction Lifecycle

Define these states and behaviours consistently for applicable business transactions.

## Draft

* A draft has no finalized stock, ledger, costing, payment, account, or reporting effect.
* An authorized user may edit or discard a draft.
* Draft invoice or purchase numbers must not be treated as posted business records unless the numbering rule explicitly reserves them.

## Posted

* Posting validates the complete transaction.
* Posting creates all required stock and financial effects atomically.
* A posted transaction is immutable.
* A posted transaction cannot be permanently deleted.
* Posted transaction snapshots must not change when product, price, unit, customer, supplier, or configuration data later changes.

## Cancelled

For Release 1, cancellation means a full corrective reversal of a posted transaction.

* The original transaction remains preserved.
* Cancellation creates a linked corrective transaction.
* Cancellation requires authorization, reason, actor, and timestamp.
* A transaction cannot be cancelled twice.
* Partial cancellation is not supported; partial correction uses a return, payment correction, or stock adjustment.
* Cancellation must fail without partial effects if the complete correction cannot be posted.

## Reversed

* A reversal is the auditable counter-transaction that neutralizes a posted transaction.
* The reversal must reference the original transaction.
* The reversal must preserve the original transaction and its snapshots.
* Reversal effects must be atomic.
* Reversal must not silently edit the original transaction.

Define which business records use:

* Draft
* Posted
* Cancelled
* Reversed

At minimum cover:

* Sale
* Purchase
* Payment
* Return
* Warehouse transfer
* Stock adjustment
* Expense
* Account transaction

# 7. Money, Quantity, and Rounding

Define these Release 1 rules:

## Money

* Release 1 business currency is PKR.
* Monetary values use two decimal places.
* Calculations must not use binary floating-point behaviour that produces inconsistent business totals.
* Intermediate calculations may retain higher precision.
* Final line and transaction amounts use deterministic round-half-up behaviour to two decimal places.
* Posted monetary values are preserved as transaction snapshots.

## Quantities

* Base-unit quantities support up to four decimal places.
* Unit-conversion factors support up to six decimal places.
* Quantities cannot be negative in user-entered transaction lines.
* Direction is represented by the transaction or stock-movement type.
* Zero-quantity posted lines are prohibited.

## Calculation order

Define a consistent calculation order for:

1. Entered packaging quantity
2. Conversion to base quantity
3. Unit price or unit cost
4. Line amount
5. Approved price adjustment where applicable
6. Transaction total
7. Payment allocation
8. Remaining receivable or payable

Do not invent taxes, GST, withholding tax, or regulatory levies.

Add tax treatment as a controlled unresolved product decision that must be resolved before purchase and sales implementation.

# 8. Product Units and Conversion

Define:

* Every stocked product has one base unit.
* Inventory is stored and reconciled in the base unit.
* A packaging unit belongs to one product.
* Every packaging unit has a positive conversion factor to the base unit.
* A conversion factor of zero or less is invalid.
* Conversions may occur only between compatible units of the same measurement dimension.
* Weight must not be converted into volume without a separately approved product-specific business rule.
* A product base unit cannot be changed after its first posted stock transaction.
* Packaging units and conversion factors may change only for future transactions.
* Posted purchases, sales, returns, opening stock, and transfers retain their original conversion snapshot.

Define the formula:

```text
Base quantity = entered packaging quantity × conversion-factor snapshot
```

# 9. Inventory and Stock Movements

Define stock as movement-derived, not a manually editable total.

## Stock formula

```text
Warehouse stock on hand
= total posted inbound base quantity
- total posted outbound base quantity
```

At minimum define movement behaviour for:

* Opening stock
* Purchase receipt
* Sale
* Sales return
* Purchase return
* Transfer outbound
* Transfer inbound
* Damage adjustment
* Expiry adjustment
* Loss adjustment
* Correction adjustment
* Cancellation
* Reversal

Rules must state:

* Every stock change requires a posted stock movement.
* Direct manual editing of the calculated stock balance is prohibited.
* Stock is scoped by organization, warehouse, product, and batch where tracking applies.
* Stock movements retain their source transaction reference.
* Cancelled or reversed source transactions are corrected through linked movements rather than deleted movements.
* Organization-level total stock excludes duplicated value during warehouse transfer.

## Negative stock

* Negative stock is blocked by default.
* Availability is validated in base units and at batch level where applicable.
* Owner override requires permission, warning, mandatory reason, and audit.
* Override applies only to the current transaction.
* Override must not disable future stock validation.
* Negative-stock sale cost uses the current warehouse-product weighted-average-cost snapshot.
* Later receipts do not retroactively change the posted cost of the negative-stock sale.

# 10. Batch and Expiry Rules

Define:

* Batch tracking is mandatory for fertilizers, seeds, pesticides, and chemicals.
* A tracked stock quantity belongs to a specific batch.
* Loose stock from different batches remains separate.
* Batch identity is preserved during purchase, sale, return, adjustment, and warehouse transfer.
* Batch number and expiry information are required according to product tracking mode.
* Manufacturing date is optional unless the applicable product or organizational rule requires it.
* Expiry is stored at batch level, not product level.

## FEFO

For products using expiry tracking:

```text
Allocate the eligible batch with the earliest expiry date first.
```

If expiry dates are equal, use the oldest received eligible stock first.

## FIFO

Where expiry does not apply:

```text
Allocate the oldest received eligible stock first.
```

## Expired stock

* A batch is expired when its expiry date is earlier than the organization’s current business date.
* Expired stock is excluded from normal sale allocation.
* Manager or Owner approval is required to sell expired stock.
* Expired sale requires warning, reason, actor, and audit entry.
* Expired stock remains separately identifiable in reporting.

# 11. Weighted-Average Cost

Weighted-average cost is maintained by:

```text
Organization + product + warehouse
```

## Purchase receipt value

```text
Receipt inventory value
= purchase-line product amount
+ allocated landed cost
```

```text
Receipt unit cost
= receipt inventory value ÷ received base quantity
```

## New weighted-average cost

For a positive receipt:

```text
New weighted-average cost
=
(existing stock value + receipt inventory value)
÷
(existing base quantity + received base quantity)
```

Define:

* Calculations use unrounded internal precision.
* The posted transaction stores the resulting cost snapshot.
* Sales use the current warehouse-product weighted-average cost at posting.
* Posted sale cost must not change retroactively.
* Warehouse transfer outbound uses source warehouse weighted-average cost.
* Warehouse transfer inbound carries the exact outbound transfer value into the destination warehouse.
* Organization-wide inventory value must not change because of a warehouse transfer.

## Landed cost

Release 1 landed-cost components may include:

* Freight
* Loading
* Transport
* Other approved non-recoverable acquisition costs

Allocate landed cost proportionally by purchase-line base product value.

If all eligible purchase-line values are zero, block posting and require manual correction rather than inventing an allocation.

Detailed tax-related landed-cost treatment remains unresolved until tax policy is approved.

# 12. Purchase Rules

Define purchase posting.

A purchase must contain:

* Organization
* Supplier
* Receiving warehouse
* At least one valid product line
* Quantity
* Unit and conversion snapshot
* Product cost
* Batch information where required
* Expiry information where required
* Landed costs where applicable

Posting a purchase atomically creates:

* Posted purchase
* Inventory receipt
* Batch stock
* Stock movements
* Weighted-average-cost update
* Supplier payable for unpaid amount
* Payment allocations
* Selected account movements
* Audit events

## Purchase payment

```text
Purchase payable
= posted purchase total
- allocated purchase payments
- supplier advances applied
- approved purchase returns or reversals
```

## Supplier invoice reference

* Supplier invoice/reference may be recorded.
* Duplicate supplier references within the same organization and supplier must produce a warning.
* Duplicate warning may be overridden only by an authorized user with reason and audit.
* A duplicate warning does not by itself prove that the purchase is a duplicate.

## Purchase cancellation

Full purchase cancellation is allowed only when:

* The purchase has not already been cancelled
* Required stock remains available for reversal
* Dependent returns or corrections do not make full reversal inconsistent
* The complete stock and financial reversal can be posted atomically

Otherwise, use purchase return or another approved corrective transaction.

# 13. Sales Rules

A sale must contain:

* Organization
* Branch
* Permitted warehouse
* At least one valid product line
* Quantity
* Unit and conversion snapshot
* Valid batch allocation where required
* Posted selling price
* Customer or approved walk-in identity
* Payment information

## Price selection

* Default unit price comes from the customer’s assigned price tier.
* Retail price is the fallback where no specific tier price exists.
* Release 1 does not provide separate line-discount or invoice-discount fields.
* Negotiated reductions use authorized price override.
* Price override requires permission, reason, actor, timestamp, and audit.
* The posted line retains the final unit price.

## Sale calculation

```text
Line total = base-equivalent sold quantity × posted unit price basis
```

The document must define the exact consistent calculation when a price is entered per packaging unit versus per base unit.

```text
Sale total = sum of posted sale-line totals
```

```text
Amount due
= sale total
- allocated payments
- customer advance applied
```

## Credit sale

* Anonymous walk-in credit is prohibited.
* Walk-in credit requires identifying customer information.
* Credit-limit behaviour is organization-configurable:

  * Warning
  * Manager approval
  * Block
* Manager approval requires reason and audit.

## Atomic sale posting

Posting creates atomically:

* Sales invoice
* Invoice sequence consumption
* Batch allocation
* Stock movements
* Cost-of-goods-sold snapshot
* Customer receivable
* Payment allocations
* Account movements
* Audit events

## Sale cancellation

Full cancellation is allowed only when:

* The sale is not already cancelled
* No unresolved linked return or correction prevents a consistent full reversal
* Required payment and account effects can be reversed
* Required stock can be restored to original batches
* The full corrective transaction can post atomically

Partial correction uses a sales return rather than partial cancellation.

# 14. Invoice Numbering

Define:

* Sales invoice numbering is branch-specific.
* Every posted sales invoice receives one unique branch sequence number.
* Drafts must not produce duplicate posted numbers.
* Failed posting must not produce two invoices with the same number.
* A cancelled invoice number is never reused.
* Sequence gaps are allowed where necessary for failure safety and auditability.
* Human-readable format may include a branch prefix and sequential number.

Exact visual formatting remains for the invoice specification task.

# 15. Payments and Allocations

## Customer payments

Support:

* Invoice-specific payment
* General payment
* Customer advance
* Application of customer advance

General customer payment allocation order:

1. Oldest due unpaid invoice
2. If due date is equal or absent, oldest invoice date
3. If invoice date is equal, lowest invoice sequence

Any remainder becomes customer advance.

## Supplier payments

Support:

* Invoice-specific supplier payment
* General supplier payment
* Supplier advance
* Application of supplier advance

General supplier payment allocation uses the oldest unpaid purchase first using the same ordering principles.

## Overpayment

* Invoice-specific payment cannot create an unexplained negative invoice balance.
* Excess approved payment becomes an advance.
* Payment allocations are preserved after posting.
* Posted payments cannot be edited.
* Payment correction uses an auditable reversal followed by a corrected payment where required.

## Payment formula

```text
Invoice outstanding
= original posted amount
- allocated payments
- applied advances
- approved returns
- reversals
```

# 16. Customer and Supplier Ledgers

Define ledgers as transaction-derived histories.

## Customer receivable

```text
Customer receivable
=
opening receivable
+ posted credit sales
- sales returns and credits
- allocated payments
- applied customer advances
± approved corrective transactions
```

A customer opening advance reduces future receivable when applied.

## Supplier payable

```text
Supplier payable
=
opening payable
+ posted unpaid purchases
- purchase returns and supplier credits
- allocated supplier payments
- applied supplier advances
± approved corrective transactions
```

Rules:

* Ledger balances must not be directly edited.
* Opening entries require auditable source transactions.
* Posted ledger entries remain historically preserved.
* Reconciliation must trace every balance component to its source.

# 17. Return Rules

## Linked sales return

Maximum returnable quantity:

```text
Original sold quantity
- previously posted non-reversed return quantity
```

For a linked sales return:

* Revenue reversal uses the original posted sale price snapshot.
* Cost reversal uses the original sale cost snapshot.
* Sellable returned stock returns to the original batch where identifiable.
* The returned quantity updates warehouse weighted-average cost using the original cost snapshot.
* Refund or ledger adjustment must match the approved returned value.

## Sales return without invoice

Requires:

* Customer lookup or recorded identifying information where available
* Manager or Owner approval
* Mandatory reason
* Manually approved refund value
* Batch identification where possible
* Audit event

Inventory value for an unlinked returned item uses the current warehouse-product weighted-average cost unless an authorized documented cost is approved.

## Purchase return

Maximum returnable quantity:

```text
Original purchased quantity
- previously posted non-reversed purchase-return quantity
```

For a linked purchase return:

* Stock is removed from the original batch where identifiable.
* Supplier payable or supplier balance is reduced by the approved return amount.
* Inventory value is removed using the original receipt cost snapshot for the returned quantity.
* The remaining warehouse weighted-average cost is recalculated from remaining stock value and quantity.

## Returned-stock condition

Classify returned stock as:

* Sellable
* Unsellable

Unsellable includes applicable:

* Expired
* Damaged
* Opened
* Contaminated
* Otherwise unsuitable for normal sale

Unsellable returned stock must not enter normal sellable inventory.

## Return resolution

Release 1 supports:

* Cash refund
* Bank, JazzCash, or Easypaisa account refund
* Customer or supplier ledger adjustment

A product exchange is represented as:

```text
Posted return
+
separate new sale
```

No separate exchange transaction type exists.

Return posting must be atomic.

# 18. Warehouse Transfer Rules

Release 1 uses a one-step atomic warehouse-transfer model.

It does not use:

* In-transit stock state
* Destination acceptance
* Separate dispatch and receipt posting

A posted transfer atomically creates:

* Outbound movement from source warehouse
* Inbound movement into destination warehouse
* Preserved product identity
* Preserved batch identity
* Preserved base quantity
* Preserved total stock value
* Audit event

Rules:

* Source and destination warehouses must differ.
* Source stock must be available.
* FEFO/FIFO does not automatically change the batch selected for an explicitly prepared transfer.
* Transfer cost uses source warehouse weighted-average-cost snapshot.
* Destination inventory receives the exact transfer value.
* Failed transfer creates neither outbound nor inbound effect.
* Organization-wide quantity and value must remain unchanged.

# 19. Accounts and Expenses

Account types:

* Cash
* Bank
* JazzCash
* Easypaisa

## Account balance

```text
Account balance
=
opening balance
+ posted inflows
- posted outflows
+ inbound transfers
- outbound transfers
+ refunds received
- refunds paid
± reversals
```

Rules:

* Account balances are transaction-derived.
* Direct balance editing is prohibited.
* Opening balances use auditable source transactions.
* Account transfer atomically creates linked outbound and inbound entries.
* Source and destination account must differ.
* Posted account movements cannot be edited or deleted.

## Expense

Posting an expense creates:

* Expense transaction
* Selected account outflow
* Audit event

Expense correction uses reversal rather than editing the posted expense.

Do not define full general-ledger entries.

# 20. Alerts

Use the organization’s configured business date and timezone.

## Low stock

```text
Low-stock condition:
sellable on-hand quantity <= configured product-and-warehouse threshold
```

## Upcoming expiry

```text
0 <= expiry date - business date <= configured expiry threshold
```

## Expired stock

```text
expiry date < business date
```

## Dead stock

A product is dead stock when:

* Sellable on-hand quantity is greater than zero
* No posted non-reversed sale occurred during the configured inactivity period

Do not silently hardcode the inactivity period.

## Dues

Customer and supplier due alerts derive from posted reconciled ledger balances.

Alerts are delivered only through:

* Dashboard
* In-app notification center

# 21. Gross-Profit Rules

Release 1 gross profit is operational gross profit, not accounting net profit.

## Net sales revenue

```text
Net sales revenue
=
posted sale revenue
- linked sales-return revenue
- cancelled or reversed sale revenue
```

Posted sale revenue uses the final authorized sale price, including any price override.

There are no separate Release 1 line-discount or invoice-discount fields.

## Net cost of goods sold

```text
Net COGS
=
posted sale cost snapshots
- linked sales-return cost reversals
- cancelled or reversed sale cost
```

## Gross profit

```text
Gross profit = net sales revenue - net COGS
```

Exclude:

* Operating expenses
* Purchase payments
* Customer payments
* Supplier payments
* Account transfers
* Full accounting adjustments
* Taxes until tax policy is approved

Dashboard and reports must use the same gross-profit calculation.

# 22. Subscription Rules

Define states:

* Pending approval
* Approved trial
* Active
* Grace
* Suspended
* Cancelled
* Retained pending deletion
* Deleted according to authorized retention process

Rules:

* Trial is available once per organization unless Super Admin explicitly approves an exception.
* Trial does not require a payment method.
* Trial duration is configurable.
* Grace duration is configurable.
* Expired active subscription enters Grace.
* Expired Grace enters Suspended.
* Suspension blocks restricted operational access according to entitlement policy.
* Suspension does not delete existing data.
* Reactivation restores entitled access.
* Cancellation and deletion are separate.
* Existing data is not deleted because a plan limit is exceeded.
* Plan-limit excess blocks only applicable new creation after warnings.
* Manual billing activation requires verified payment evidence and authorized approval.

Exact prices and commercial defaults remain outside P1-03.

# 23. Import Rules

Supported imports remain those listed in the frozen PRD.

Define:

* Every import uses a defined template version.
* The system validates all rows before posting.
* Invalid rows identify row and field.
* Invalid rows are not silently ignored.

* An import posting operation is logically all-or-nothing.
* An unrecoverable posting failure must not leave a partially committed import.
* Import correction uses auditable reversal or approved correction rather than direct deletion.
